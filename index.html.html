FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a ticket? +
You can book through our website using the search form, call our helpline at +92 300 1234567, WhatsApp us, or visit any of our terminals directly. Online booking system is coming soon.
Can I cancel or change my booking? +
Yes. Cancellations made 24+ hours before departure receive a full refund. Changes can be made up to 6 hours before departure subject to seat availability. Please call our helpline to make changes.
How much luggage can I carry? +
Each passenger is allowed one piece of luggage (up to 20kg) in the cargo hold and one carry-on bag (up to 7kg). Extra luggage is charged at Rs 200 per kg.
Are student discounts available? +
Yes! Students with a valid university/college ID get a 15% discount on all routes. Senior citizens (60+) receive a 10% discount. Children under 5 travel free when accompanied by an adult.
Is food provided on long-distance routes? +
Sleeper coaches on overnight routes include one meal. AC coaches make scheduled stops at dhaba/restaurants. Standard buses make roadside stops. Passengers are welcome to bring their own food.
How early should I arrive at the terminal? +
We recommend arriving at least 30 minutes before departure for standard buses, and 45 minutes for sleeper/AC coaches to allow time for boarding and luggage loading.
